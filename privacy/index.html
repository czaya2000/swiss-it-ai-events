Privacy

Short version: no cookies, no tracking across sites, no selling of anything. Here is the whole picture, as required by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Who is responsible

Marcin Czajkowski, Geneva, Switzerland — czajkowski81@gmail.com. See the Impressum.

Just browsing

The site is static and sets no cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and does not build a profile of you: it records aggregate page views, referrers, country and browser type, without storing your IP address. The site's typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests the font files from Google's servers; Google may record the request under its own privacy policy. Hosting is on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which keeps standard server logs.

Links to organisers

Registration buttons link to the organiser's own page and carry a tag (utm_source=swissdataevents.ch) so organisers can see that a visitor came from here. Nothing about you is sent — only that this site was the referrer. What the organiser's page does with your data is governed by their privacy policy, not this one.

The weekly digest

If you subscribe, your email address is used for one thing: sending you the weekly digest of new Swiss data and AI events. It is never sold, shared or added to anything else. Unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email or by writing to the address above, and it is deleted.

Contact and event-submission forms

Forms are relayed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards what you typed to the inbox above and keeps nothing beyond what it needs to deliver the message. Your details are used only to reply to you or to list the event you submitted. Organiser names on event listings are public information taken from the organiser's own page.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, have it corrected or deleted, at any time — one email is enough. If sponsorships or paid listings are ever introduced, this page will be updated first.
