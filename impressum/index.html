What this site is
A privately run, non-commercial directory of data, AI and IT events in Switzerland. Event details are collected from organisers' public pages and checked by hand, but they can change on the organiser's side at any time — always confirm on the organiser's own page before you travel or pay. Swiss Data Events sells no tickets and takes no commission.
Liability for external links
Every listing links to a third-party page. Those pages are the responsibility of their operators; Swiss Data Events has no influence over their content and accepts no liability for it.
